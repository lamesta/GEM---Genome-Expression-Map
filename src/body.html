How to use

Circle: mouse wheel or trackpad pinch zooms, drag pans, double-click zooms in (shift + double-click zooms out). Shift + drag (or “select arc”) selects an arc for the linear view; a single click centres a 30 kb window. Gene labels appear along the rim when zoomed in. The linear view follows the ring selection; brush its gene track to zoom further. Hover any bar or gene for details.
